# Protocol excerpt (demo study)

> This is a demonstration based on the public CDISC pilot study data as
> regenerated by the pharmaverse. It contains no real patient data.

## Study design

A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group
study of the Xanomeline transdermal therapeutic system (TTS) in subjects
with mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease.

Subjects were randomized 1:1:1 to one of three arms:

- **Placebo** — placebo transdermal patch
- **Xanomeline Low Dose** — Xanomeline TTS 54 cm2
- **Xanomeline High Dose** — Xanomeline TTS 81 cm2

Planned treatment duration is 26 weeks, with visits at Weeks 2, 4, 6, 8,
12, 16, 20, 24, and 26.

## Safety objectives

Safety and tolerability are assessed through adverse events, clinical
laboratory evaluations (chemistry, hematology, urinalysis), vital signs,
and skin evaluation at the patch application site.

Adverse events of special interest include application-site reactions
(pruritus, erythema, dermatitis) and cholinergic events (nausea, vomiting,
dizziness, sweating), consistent with the known pharmacology of xanomeline,
a muscarinic agonist.

## Adverse event definitions

An **adverse event (AE)** is any untoward medical occurrence in a subject
administered study treatment, regardless of causal relationship.

A **serious adverse event (SAE)** is any AE that results in death, is
life-threatening, requires or prolongs inpatient hospitalization, results
in persistent or significant disability/incapacity, or is a congenital
anomaly/birth defect.

AE severity is graded as MILD, MODERATE, or SEVERE. Severity is distinct
from seriousness.
